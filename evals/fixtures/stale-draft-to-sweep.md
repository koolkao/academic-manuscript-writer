# Preoperative Gabapentinoid Tapering and 90-Day Persistent Opioid Use

## Key Points
**Findings:** Among 800 patients undergoing total knee arthroplasty, preoperative tapering was
associated with a −7.8 percentage point lower 90-day persistent opioid rate (P = .004).

## Abstract
Among 800 chronic gabapentinoid users (310 taper, 490 usual care), preoperative tapering reduced
90-day persistent opioid use by 7.8 percentage points (95% CI, −13.1 to −2.5; P = .004).

## Methods
After exclusions, 800 patients remained (310 taper, 490 usual care; Table 1).

## Results
Across 800 patients, 90-day persistent opioid use differed by −7.8 percentage points (95% CI,
−13.1 to −2.5; P = .004; Table 1).

**Table 1.** Cohort summary (n = 800).

| Metric | Taper (n = 310) | Usual Care (n = 490) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90-day persistent opioid use, No. (%) | 57 (18.4) | 132 (26.9) |
