# Fixture: Raw Project Notes & Tables (No Clear Framing)

Project: Retrospective study of preoperative gabapentinoid tapering protocols before total knee
arthroplasty (TKA) across 1 tertiary hospital (2020–2024).

Notes (lab chronology):
- First we pulled 910 chronic gabapentinoid users undergoing primary TKA.
- Excluded 62 revisions and 48 with missing baseline PROMIS pain interference -> 800 patients.
- 310 patients enrolled in pharmacist-guided preoperative taper (4 weeks pre-op); 490 continued
  usual baseline dose.
- Primary 90-day outcome: persistent opioid use at 90 days post-op: 18.4% taper vs 26.9% usual
  (IPTW adjusted RD -7.8%, 95% CI -13.1% to -2.5%, P = .004).
- Secondary: 90-day inpatient delirium: 3.2% taper vs 6.5% usual (RD -3.1%, 95% CI -5.9% to -0.3%).
- Secondary: 90-day knee pain NRS score: 3.4 vs 3.5 (diff -0.1, 95% CI -0.4 to 0.2, P = .52) ->
  tapering did NOT worsen postoperative pain!
- Subgroup: patients on >=1800 mg/day gabapentin equivalent had biggest opioid drop (-12.4%) vs
  low-dose (<900 mg/day) (-2.1%; interaction P = .02).
- We did NOT collect 12-month readmission costs or pharmacy labor hours.
- One coauthor wants to claim "preoperative tapering saves hospitals money and cures post-TKA
  opioid dependence."
